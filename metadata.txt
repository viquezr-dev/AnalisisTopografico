description=Topographic leveling network adjustment by least squares (Gaus-Markov method).
about=QGIS plugin for adjusting topographic leveling networks by the least squares method (Gaus-Markov). It performs data validation, computes adjusted elevations, residuals, posteriori variance, chi-square test, confidence intervals and Baarda's data snooping, and generates a professional HTML report with a scaled network sketch. Supports optional UTM coordinates to display results on the map. Exports results to GeoPackage and CSV.
